Privacy Policy

How RENVI handles your personal data. Version of 4 November 2026.

Who we are

RENVI is a dating app operated by World Work, s.r.o., Družstevná 543, 956 19 Krnča, Slovakia (company no. 51661136). This policy explains what data the RENVI app collects and why. The GoApps Lab website has its own, separate notice. For any data request, use the support page.

What we collect

  • Account: your e-mail address and a password (stored only as a hash).
  • Profile: name, date of birth, gender, who you are looking for, city and country, intent, your answers to prompts, and the optional details you add (height, languages, children, smoking and so on).
  • Photos and video: profile photos, short videos (up to 15 seconds) and a selfie used to verify your profile.
  • Location: approximate location, only if you allow it. It is rounded and used to show distance. Other people never see your exact position.
  • Activity: sparks, matches, messages, date invitations, reports, blocks and appeals.
  • Device: a push-notification token, if you allow notifications.
  • Usage analytics: only if you give consent in the app. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Technical errors: diagnostic information about crashes and failures.

Why we use it

To create and show your profile, suggest people, deliver messages, keep the service safe (reports, blocking, photo verification, filtering of banned words and spam), send notifications you asked for, fix errors, and meet legal obligations. Our legal bases are performing our contract with you, your consent (analytics, location, notifications), our legitimate interest in safety and preventing abuse, and legal obligation.

Who sees your data

Other users see only what you put on your profile and your age, never your date of birth. Our moderators can see reported content and account data needed to handle a report. We use service providers who process data on our behalf: Supabase (database, storage, sign-in), Apple (push notifications), an e-mail delivery provider for confirmation and reset messages and, if you use it, a video-call provider for pre-date calls. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, your profile, photos, messages and related data are deleted; limited copies may remain in backups for a short time. Usage analytics are deleted automatically after 180 days. Records of moderation decisions may be kept as long as needed to prevent abuse and defend legal claims.

Your rights

You can access, correct and delete your data, restrict or object to processing, take a copy, and withdraw consent. In the app: Settings lets you download a copy of your data, delete your account, withdraw analytics consent, and block or report anyone. Your date of birth and gender are fixed after sign-up; contact support if one is wrong. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Age

RENVI is only for people aged 18 or older. We delete accounts we find belong to someone under 18.

Security and changes

Data is encrypted in transit, access to rows is limited to their owner and moderators, and administrator actions are logged. If we make material changes to this policy we will tell you in the app.
