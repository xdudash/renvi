Community Guidelines

How we keep RENVI respectful and safe. Version of 4 November 2026.

Be respectful

Treat people the way you want to be treated. A no is a complete answer. Do not keep pushing.

Be real

Use your own photos, where you are visible. No photos of children as your main photo, no other people's photos, no generated faces.

No harassment

Threats, bullying, discrimination of any kind and unwanted sexual content break the rules.

No selling or scams

No advertising, links to other services, or requests for money or gifts.

Stay safe

Do not share your address, documents or payment details. Report anything suspicious with the Report button in a profile or chat. You can block anyone at any time.

How we respond

A warning first for lesser issues, then a temporary suspension, then a permanent ban. Serious violations go straight to a ban. Every decision can be appealed in the app and the answer appears on the same screen.

How we review

Moderators review reports and decisions are logged. Automatic filters stop banned words and repeated mass messages. We aim to look at reports within 24 hours.
